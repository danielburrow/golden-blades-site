How joining works

  1. Find a point of recruitment. One of our flyers or any officer will do. You will recognize them.
  2. Sign on as a contractor. Tell them you want to sign up as a contractor or sub-contractor, or to offer another skill to the Blades. You will be shown the Regola Condotta and given a contract.
  3. Work your first contracts. Side jobs alongside full members show everyone you know which end of the sword is dangerous.
  4. Ask for a full enlistment contract. It lasts until the next Grand Gathering, and you can re-enlist for the year.
  5. Face the Court of Pikes. With mutual agreement, an officer brings you before the Court and vouches for you. If there are no objections, you are sworn in under a hedge of pikes.

What we look for

  • Good conduct out of character. You can be goofy, but you act like an adult in chat and in person.
  • A good fit in character. Would your character have a good time and plenty of roleplay with us?
  • Maturity. Can you separate LARP from the real world, and step back if bleed gets heavy?
  • A sense of fun. Can you enjoy not being the center of the storyline?
  • Good vibes. Do you put the feeling of being a character, and everyone having a good time, above winning?
