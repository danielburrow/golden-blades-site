The Phighting Phoenixes

The Golden Blades took the Maceball pitch in the tournament at FoC in May 2026 as the Phighting Phoenixes, the team of Lord DeVry’s University of the Phoenix. Lord DeVry paid the team’s entry fee. The players wear numbered tabards in blue and yellow, with matching face paint.
