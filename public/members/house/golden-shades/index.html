Golden Shades

The ghosts of Golden Blades who failed to uphold their contracts.

Who they are

The Golden Shades are the ghosts of Golden Blades who failed to uphold their contracts. The tale goes that they were formed from broken contracts and mercenary tears, and that they still carry their debts. Among Blades they are best played as silly and mostly harmless, a ghost in a fancy hat more than a real menace.

A Shade can be saved from their debts, according to the rumor, by leaving a coin offering at the shrine in the Golden Blades Palazzo.

Shade duty

At events, Blades take shade duty: playing ghosts for the story team when it is short on shades. You do not need to sign up ahead of time. Show up at story, and if they are lacking shades you can step in. It is extra paid work for a Blade, and a good way to spend time while you are dead.

The costume is a sheet or a length of gold fabric over the head, with eye holes, and most Shades add a hat. Bring your own sheet.
