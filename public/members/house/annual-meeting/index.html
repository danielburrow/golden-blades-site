Annual Shareholders’ Meeting

Drill, dinner, and the yearly business of the regiment.

What it is

Once a year we hold a Shareholder’s Meeting, with a drill session and dinner when it is held in person. It is where economic decisions and minor business are settled. Dress is casual, and questionable sobriety is expected.

Every Blade is a shareholder, and stock decides voting power. Citizen card counts break ties. For the full rules see Pay, Contracts & Stock and Meetings & Customs in the Primer.

The Annual Shareholder Survey

Before the meeting everyone fills out the anonymous Annual Shareholder Survey, run by the Personalreferent. It sets the agenda, so speak up.
