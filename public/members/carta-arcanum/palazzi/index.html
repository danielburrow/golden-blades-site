The Palazzo Program

Neutral fortress-embassies across the Known World, run like branches of an international company.

What a Palazzo is

The Golden Blades are not a kingdom, but we do business with all of them. A Palazzo is our answer: a fortified office we hold inside someone else's territory. It was first pitched as a unique building that could grow into a trade hall and a fort. The full name was proposed as the "Palazzo dell'Arte della Guerra," and most are called by the name of their place, such as Palazzo di Nathair.

  • A branch office. Think of the Palazzi as branches of an international corporation. Clients meet the Blades there on an official basis, hire contracts, and trade.
  • Neutral ground. A Palazzo is meant to be neutral. Even if the Blades fight a kingdom, the holding in its land acts as an embassy where hostilities can be negotiated away. Disputing parties can use the Palazzo to mediate, with the Blades guaranteeing protection for a fee.
  • Credibility. An office in every region makes us safer working with everyone, and makes us credible as a neutral party. Our reputation for deals and notarization helps the Blades broker peace talks, and the Blades are the forces both sides pay to keep them orderly.
  • In the game. Mechanically it is a fort we own on land that belongs to someone else, which may mean paying a little extra upkeep to our landlord. A kingdom that hosts a Palazzo was proposed to swap the standard mercenary action for the Golden Blades.
  • An alliance. A Palazzo bordering a nation means we have brokered some alliance with it.

What they look like

Every Palazzo is built to the same recipe: a fortress dressed as a place of business, flying gold and blue.

Palazzo di Nathair

It stands on the northern edge of the city in white limestone with red brick and a red tile roof, more a fortress than an office. Gold and blue banners hang down its blank walls, crossbowmen patrol the roof, and two guards in flamboyant uniform flank the only door. It opens onto a plaza of yellow and red bricks forming snarling griffons around the Fountain of Fortune, a marble grotto topped with bronze figures in esoteric armor. Merchants the Blades have licensed trade there. Inside, a great meeting hall has a blue marble floor, iron chandeliers, and a mezzanine with sword-shaped railings. The shields of the Great Companies hang from it in hammered bronze, and frescoes of battles line the walls.

Palazzo di Vandor

A lighter, more elegant building in white stone and pale blue marble, with a round domed central hall and two smaller towers with emerald roofs. Its plaza of pale blue paving surrounds a reflecting pool with a brass tree with gold leaves at its center. Pennants of blue and gold fly from each arch.

Inside any Palazzo

  • Ground floor: a hall for members and clients, with the heraldry of the Great Companies and frescoes of Blades exploits.
  • Upper floors: barracks, training rooms, and offices, each tailored by its occupant.
  • Top floor: a casino for trusted associates, rich with wood inlay and velvet in regimental colors.

Palazzi at events

At Hynafol events, the Blades also build their own Palazzo in camp, usually as a tent or pavilion dressed in blue and gold. The Blades even hand out a "Most Bestest Palazzo" award. Building and dressing it is a team effort, so ask in the Blades Discord if you want to help.
