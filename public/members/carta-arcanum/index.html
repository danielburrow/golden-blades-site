What is the Carta Arcanum?

The Carta Arcanum is the map-and-resources game that runs underneath Hynafol's events. Houses claim and settle land in hexes, build on it, pay upkeep, gather resources, raise armies and units, and send ships and caravans out to trade. Between events, nothing stands still. A ship that sails off visits its ports and brings back goods, and the world on the map shifts with every expedition.

For the Blades it is also where the money is. Our nobility, our Internal Stock Market, and our Palazzi all grow out of it. Everything about that game, and about the world it is played on, lives in this section. Everything else about us as a house is under House.

The Hex Ledger

The Hex Ledger is a website built by one of our own Blades, Brenin Halvard, where every Golden Blade pools what they know about the map, plans trade routes, and fills in dispatch forms. Its companion, the Holdings Ledger, helps plan what to build on your hexes.
