The Hex Ledger

A shared map register and route planner built by one of our own.

The Hex Ledger is a website built by one of our own Blades, Brenin Halvard, that lets every Golden Blade pool what they know about the map. It answers questions the printed maps do not: which ports exist, what they produce, whether they have a lighthouse, and who can be trusted to let your ships through.

Open the Hex Ledger

What it does

  • Hex Register. A shared record of hexes, with their House and Kingdom, buildings, and details. Anyone with an account can add hexes they know about, and every change is tracked so a bad edit can be reverted.
  • Trade routes. A route advisor, called the Counsel, proposes voyages. It weighs expected calamities against your ship's defense and labels each leg, for example "Warded" when your defenses cover the risk. You can declare the flag you fly, and it avoids ports known to be hostile to that color. Buildings on a hex weight the trade, so three Orchards yield more Wood than one.
  • Safe Passage Register. After each voyage, report which flag colors passed safely, or hostilely, through which ports. The Counsel uses those reports in future proposals.
  • Transport Dispatch Form. Save a route as a charter, and the Ledger fills in the official Carta dispatch form for you and downloads it as a PDF, ready to submit. Charters can be kept private, which hides them from everyone but you and regimental staff.
  • My Profile. Set your character name, House, Kingdom, and your roster of ships and caravans, and they autofill your dispatch forms. Your legal name stays private.
  • World Map. A painted map with every hex labeled and clickable, colored by House. Click a hex for a summary card and a picture of what is built there. If a register entry has no place yet, use Pick and click the map to assign it.

Getting in

Create an account on the site. Access is for Golden Blades only, and new accounts are approved by hand, so you may need to ask in the Blades Discord. Ask the Ledger's keeper if something looks wrong.

Hex Ledger and map data are House-sensitive. Please do not share them, or screenshots of them, outside the Golden Blades.

The Holdings Ledger

Its companion tool plans holdings rather than routes. It helps you work out what to build on your hexes. The Treasurer's tab fills in the forms for you from the names of your hexes, and a Requisitions tab handles requests.
