Land and Holdings

How the Blades turn coin into land, and how that land is organized.

The Land Development Program

Announced in October 2024 and open to all full members of the Golden Blades. Mercenaries live short lives, and the program exists so that what a Blade earns can become something lasting for the family and friends they leave behind. Stock in the regiment can be cashed out for money and cards as the program lays out. Members use it to turn winnings into settled land and buildings.

The rules that now govern it are in Briefadel and Ownership.

How it works in practice

  • Plan on the map. The Holdings Ledger helps you work out which hexes and buildings to go for.
  • Settle and build. New land becomes a hex with a name. Buildings on it produce resources, and those resources are weighed in the trade lottery.
  • Pay upkeep. Upkeep on buildings and hexes is paid to the Treasurer before Grand Gathering.
  • Collect. Output is collected from the Treasurer on the first day of an event. After the deadline it is treated as donated to the House.
  • Mind the map. Some hexes carry calamities that can hit armies or ships, so check the Hex Ledger before you commit.
