The full story

Open a reign to read what happened. As told by Danen Zampfer.

Kaiser Ulric ISept 12, 810 AC – Sept 12, 810 ACElected with no documented proclamation. Slain the same day by a Vermiscar assassin.

The shortest reign on record. Nobody wrote down a proclamation, and the assassin from Vermiscar struck before the day was out.

Kaiserine Ibb ISept 12, 810 AC – Nov 10, 810 ACFirst words as Kaiserine: “Thank y—” (interrupted by Athame). Squished by her bodyguards.

Ibb was elected at the close of Courtly Nights, and her first words as Kaiserine, “Thank y—”, were interrupted by Athame.

Kaiserine Ibb fought grandly at the Hunt in October, but she lost her life in the first Grand Battle. She was crushed in the pike block between her own heavily armored guards. Reports from the front say her last words were squeaks. No remains were found, but the crown was recovered.

Kaiser Rothwyr INov 10, 810 AC – Nov 12, 810 ACFirst words as Kaiser: “I don’t know what I’m doing.” Died of food poisoning from undercooked chicken.

After Ibb’s death the Elector Counts were summoned and Parliament was held. Then came a wonderful event: two new Houses were inducted into the Holy Reman League, the dark knights of Vekmarch and the proud viking clans of Sleipnir’s Rise.

The Elector Counts then voted for a new Kaiser. Danen Zampfer received three votes, but his brother from Vekmarch, a strong knight known as Rothwyr, won with four. Rothwyr was in the latrines at the time of the vote. He returned to Parliament as Kaiser, and his first words were “I don’t know what I’m doing.”

Two days later, Rothwyr died of food poisoning from undercooked chicken. Just as he was elected, he died in the latrines.

Kaiser Tal INov 12, 810 AC – Nov 14, 810 ACFirst words as Kaiser: “Uhh.” Slain while defying Parliament, by Coen Sunderr.

The electors were summoned again, and Tal of Sleipnir’s Rise was elected.

During Parliament, it was decided that the Holy Reman League would fight alongside the Kingdom of the Tides. This was a predicament for the new Kaiser, who had an outstanding contract to fight for Equinox in the final grand battle. So on Saturday the entire League took the field, armed to the teeth, and across from them stood Kaiser Tal.

Among the Golden Blades, a betting pool had formed on when Tal’s reign would end. To everyone’s surprise, Tal survived the first few skirmishes and cost many Golden Blades their money.

Tal was eventually slain by a renowned Militum archer named Coen. By rights, the crown passed to Coen, though he didn’t know it. The Golden Blades, longtime rivals of Militum, groaned and wailed in dismay.

Kaiser Coen INov 14, 810 AC – Nov 14, 810 ACFirst words as Kaiser: “I guess I have an hour.” Slain by Abigail.

Then Lorros Greyfeather, warrior of Vekmarch, dueled the revived Kaiser Tal and took the crown for himself. Everyone rejoiced, but the succession was now in dispute.

After the battle and the great victory, an emergency session of the Elector Counts was called. Due to his rightful claim, Militum’s Coen was given the crown.

Kaiser Coen lived for 61 minutes, until Countess Abigail slit his throat and returned the crown to the Holy Reman League. The crown was then put away for cleaning, as it reeked of Militum.

Today, no Kaiser oversees the Holy Reman League.

The crown rests in the care of the Holy Reman League. No Kaiser has been seated since.
