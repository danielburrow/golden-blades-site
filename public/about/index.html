Who we are

The Golden Blades are the most professional mercenaries in the Known World of Hynafol, and we make sure everyone knows it.

We treat soldiering for hire as a business. Contracts are formal, paperwork matters, and a signed contract is a promise. We are lively, good-humored, and slow to hold grudges. Our stated goals are earning coin and enjoying it, because mercenary work is dangerous and wealth is meant to be spent.

Anton von Forst, a veteran sellsword, formed the first version of the regiment in 807 A.C. to collect battlefield loot. After a treasure find and a lot of camaraderie it became the Golden Blades. We are members of the Holy Reman League, our House's Kingdom.

How we look

Colors

Goldenrod and dark blue. If it is blue and gold, it is ours.

Style

Fifteenth and sixteenth century Italian condottieri, Swiss troops, and Holy Roman Empire Landsknechts. Flashy clothing, jewelry, sashes, and plumes.

Companies

Five Great Companies give each Blade an identity and some flavor for their roleplay, though nothing is set in stone.

The Regola Condotta

Every Blade lives by six rules.

  • Contratto (Contract). A mercenary follows contracts.
  • Azienda (Company). A mercenary is loyal to their company.
  • Denaro (Coin). A mercenary fights for coin, not for ideals, nations, or people.
  • Missione (The Job). A mercenary prioritizes the mission.
  • Rancori (Grudges). A mercenary conducts business on the battlefield, not score-settling.
  • Sopravvivenza (Survival). A mercenary's legacy is survival.

Voluntary by design

Membership is voluntary, and the contractual nature of the Blades means you can negotiate your exit at any time. Your voice matters from day one, so we want to hear your concerns and ideas about the direction and culture of the regiment.
